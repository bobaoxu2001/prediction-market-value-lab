See your first live result in under five minutes.

Open one Kalshi or Polymarket contract, choose a side, and enter an amount. PMVL will put the estimated entry cost beside the order ticket.

First success

One contract. One amount. One clear cost.

  1. 01

    Open a single contract

    On a multi-outcome board, select the exact contract first so its YES/NO order ticket is visible.

  2. 02

    Choose YES or NO and enter an amount

    PMVL reads the selected side and amount. It never types into the ticket and you do not need to submit an order.

  3. 03

    Look beside the order ticket

    The panel shows estimated cost per contract, break-even probability, quote age, and a lower-cost placeable size when one exists.

Read-only by construction

The extension calculates. You decide.

  • Reads only public Kalshi and Polymarket market data.
  • Does not request your wallet, venue login, tabs, or browsing history.
  • Does not place an order or change an order ticket.
  • Runs its cost calculation locally in the extension.

If the panel is not visible

Refresh the market tab once after installation.

Then open a specific contract and make sure its order ticket is on screen. Board and event pages without a selected outcome intentionally show no estimate—the extension will not guess which contract you mean.

You can reopen this guide at any time by selecting the PMVL toolbar icon.
